A Value for value experience

V4V Chicago is a two-day gathering for builders, creators, and freedom lovers in the Bitcoin community. Hosted by Chicago Bitcoin Collective and Fork & Coin, this event is build on the spirt of Value for Value.

We believe in exchanging value freely -- through time, talent, and tressure. V4V Chicago brings together the best of Bitcoin culture with workshops, music, great food, and a vibrant community.

What is value for value?

Value for Value (V4V) is an economic model that supports creators and communities by aligning incentives through direct value exchange. At V4V Chicago, we live it -- not just talk it.

Here are some common questions you might have. If you have further questions about the event, feel free to reach out to use at: info@forkandcoin.com

When is the event?
V4V Chicago takes place September 14–15, 2026.
Where is the event?
Fork and Coin - 3938 N Central Ave, Chicago, IL 60634
Who is this event for?
V4V Chicago is for Bitcoiners, builders, creators, small business owners, artists, freedom tech users, and anyone interested in real-world Bitcoin adoption and community.
What is the Bitcoin Bodega?
The Bitcoin Bodega is CBC’s member marketplace — a place where Bitcoiners can discover, support, and shop directly with other Bitcoiners.
Will there be food and drinks?
Yes. Fork & Coin will host dinner and drinks on Monday, with lunch and snacks included for full stack pass
Do I need to be technical to attend?
No. V4V Chicago is designed to be welcoming and practical for both experienced Bitcoiners and people who are newer to Bitcoin, Nostr, and freedom tech.
