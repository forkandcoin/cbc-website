The Chicago Bitcoin Collective (CBC) is Chicago’s dedicated Bitcoin community hub — a place for builders, educators, students, and enthusiasts who take Bitcoin seriously.

We Built the Room That Was Missing 

Bitcoin has no shortage of content. Podcasts, Twitter threads, Discord channels — the information is everywhere. What Chicago didn't have was a physical place where long-term Bitcoin thinkers could sit across from each other.

Introduction

What is the Chicago Bitcoin Collective?

The Chicago Bitcoin Collective (CBC) is Chicago’s dedicated Bitcoin community hub — a place for builders, educators, students, and enthusiasts who take Bitcoin seriously.

We exist to:

01.
Unite

Bring Chicago Bitcoiners together around shared purpose.

02.
Educate

Host events and discussions that deepen understanding of Bitcoin’s monetary, economic, and cultural impact.

03.
Collaborate

Create space for builders to work, connect, and launch ideas aligned with Bitcoin’s long-term future.

How it works

The Chicago Bitcoin Collective operates on a simple idea: value for value.

Our Offer

01.
Community

A welcoming network of Bitcoiners from all backgrounds — whether you’re new to BTC or have been stacking sats for years.

02.
Space

Fork and Coin - A central, secure space designed for coworking, thoughtful work, and intentional connection.

03.
Events

Regular in-person meetups, talks, and community gatherings focused on Bitcoin education and real-world dialogue.

Our Ask

Our members support the Collective in return — not just financially, but through contribution. Whether that be with your Time, Talent, or Treasure; There’s no single “right” way to contribute.

MISSION STATEMENT

WE ARE HERE To create and sustain the physical home for Bitcoiners in Chicago. a place where people who take Bitcoin seriously can think, build, and connect in person.

WHY PHYSICAL SPACE MATTERS

Bitcoin Is Digital.
‍Community Isn't.

There's something that can't be replicated online: the friction of a real conversation. The moment when a question gets answered face-to-face. The connection made over coffee that turns into a collaboration.

Physical community is how trust is built. It's how accountability works. It's how real things get made. CBC exists in Chicago because Chicago's Bitcoin community deserves a home — not just a hashtag.

What we Stand for

01

Bitcoin-Only

We are explicitly and unapologetically Bitcoin-only. No altcoins. No tokens. No 'crypto.' CBC is a signal-rich environment built around the one monetary network with the fundamentals to matter long-term. This isn't a political statement — it's a focus decision.
02

Low Time Preference

We think in years and decades, not cycles. CBC is not a bull-market reaction. It's a long-term institution built
to compound over time. Members who thrive here are patient builders, not trend-chasers.
03

Value-for-Value

CBC operates on the principle that value flows in both directions. Members receive access, community, and programming — and they support the Collective through time, talent, and financial contribution. This is not charity. It's not a transaction. It's a community.
04

Signal Over Noise

Our events, conversations, and content are curated for depth, not virality. Members come here to think clearly — not to be overwhelmed.
05

Open but Focused

We welcome newcomers, skeptics, and curious minds at every stage of their Bitcoin journey. But we are not a general crypto community. Our openness has a direction — toward greater Bitcoin understanding and adoption.
