Introduction

What is the Chicago Bitcoin Collective?

The Chicago Bitcoin Collective (CBC) is Chicago’s dedicated Bitcoin community hub — a place for builders, educators, students, and enthusiasts who take Bitcoin seriously.

We exist to:

Unite

Bring Chicago Bitcoiners together around shared purpose.

Educate

Host events and discussions that deepen understanding of Bitcoin’s monetary, economic, and cultural impact.

Collaborate

Create space for builders to work, connect, and launch ideas aligned with Bitcoin’s long-term future.

Our Ask

Our members support the Collective in return — not just financially, but through contribution. Whether that be with your Time, Talent, or Treasure; There’s no single “right” way to contribute.

FORK & COIN

Fork & Coin is the bar and venue located within the CBC building. Fork & Coin isn't a separate destination — it's part of the CBC experience. The bar operates within our walls and serves the community we've built here.

‍

  • Full bar and beverage service
  • Hours: Wednesday to Saturday, 11 AM to 9 PM. Sunday, 11 AM to 3 PM. Closed Monday and Tuesday.
  • Member discounts apply (5–15% based on membership tier)
  • Available for private event buyouts — inquire with the CBC team
  • 3938 N Central Ave, Chicago. Visit forkandcoin.com.
